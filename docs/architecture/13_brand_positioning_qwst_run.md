# 13. Brand Strategy, Identity & Naming Architecture: QWST.RUN

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Positioning

The platform is officially repositioned from a reactive "anti-scroll" utility to an affirmative, kinetic participation platform:

> **QWST.RUN = A platform for turning intent into participation.**

The "anti-scroll" concept is preserved strictly as a **cultural contrast**, not the product definition. QWST serves individuals, university campuses, companies, creators, and community organizers. A product built around deprivation ("don't scroll") cannot scale to enterprise or civic institutions; a product built around **execution and participation** ("run your world") can.

---

## 2. Core Philosophy & Value Proposition

* **Philosophy Tagline:** *“Life isn't a feed. It's something you run.”*
* **Mission:** Bridging the gap between digital discovery and real-world kinetic participation.
* **Core Distinction:** Feeds optimize for passive dwell time; QWST optimizes for active real-world presence, relational depth, and skill acquisition.

---

## 3. Brand Hierarchy & Semantic Architecture

```text
                           QWST (The Ecosystem)
                                     │
                           RUN (The Kinetic Layer)
                                     │
           ┌─────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────┐
         QUEST                     PLACES                    PEOPLE
      (Action Unit)              (Anchors)                 (Identities)
           │                         │                         │
           └─────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────┘
                                     │
                           GROWTH & REPUTATION
```

* **QWST:** The overarching brand and ecosystem.
* **RUN:** The kinetic participation layer (action verb and digital address).
* **QUEST:** The fundamental atomic unit of experience (any intentional commitment that asks someone to show up and participate).
* **PLACES & PEOPLE:** Spatial nodes (Hubs, Campuses, Venues) and Identity Genomes.
* **GROWTH & REPUTATION:** The output of verified behavior, trust scores, and server-backed XP.

## 4. Brand Configurations: Options, Alternatives & Verdict

Three primary configurations were evaluated:

### Configuration A: The Minimalist Utility
* **Structure:** `QWST` with `qwst.run` as a background domain.
* **Pros:** Clean, minimal, tech-forward.
* **Cons:** Passive; treats "RUN" merely as a web URL rather than an action verb. Lacks an emotional consumer rallying cry.
* **Verdict:** 🟡 Sub-optimal.

### Configuration B: The Domain-First Kinetic Name
* **Structure:** `QWST.RUN` as the single unified compound name (*“Run your world.”*).
* **Pros:** High energy; inseparable from the participation verb.
* **Cons:** High consumer friction; reads like a fitness tracking app (Nike Run Club, Strava) or a dev utility.
* **Verdict:** 🟡 Ambiguous category anchor.

### Configuration C: The Multi-Tier Brand Architecture (SELECTED VERDICT)
* **Structure:**
  - **Brand:** `QWST` *(The memorable identity people speak and share)*
  - **Rallying Cry:** `RUN YOUR WORLD.` *(The emotional and behavioral promise)*
  - **Digital Anchor:** `qwst.run` *(The unique universal link and web destination)*
  - **Atomic Experience:** `QUEST` *(The structured unit of participation)*
* **Pros:**
  1. Solves the Meta Quest (Oculus) App Store collision by owning **QWST**.
  2. Anchors the word "run" to human agency, campus life, and social momentum rather than athletic jogging.
  3. Seamlessly integrates with universal deep-linking (`qwst.run/q/:id`, `qwst.run/e/:id`).
* **Verdict:** 🟢 **SUPERIOR & APPROVED**.

## 5. Product Action Language Taxonomy

QWST replaces generic CRUD social buttons with kinetic, verb-based actions:

| Standard Social Term | QWST Language | Context & Mental Model |
|---|---|---|
| Start / Accept Mission | **RUN QUEST** | Activates an intentional real-world challenge or track. |
| Host / Launch Event | **RUN EVENT** | Host/Creator view for publishing and managing gatherings. |
| Accept / RSVP Event | **JOIN / ATTEND** | Attendee participation contract (avoids confusing attendee with host). |
| Create Group | **RUN COMMUNITY** | Organizer governance and leadership. |
| Squad / Co-op Action | **RUN WITH FRIENDS** | Social cohort invitations, group quests, and collective check-ins. |
| Check-in Proximity | **RUN PRESENCE** | Activating radar and physical hub check-ins. |

## 7. Aesthetic Guardrails: Preventing the "Hacker Terminal" Trap

* **Brand Layer:** Monospace accents (`QWST.RUN`, tabular XP meters, subtle `$ run_quest` telemetry) are permitted in micro-copy and badge headers.
* **Consumer UI Layer:** The primary application interface must remain **warm, tactile, approachable, and visually premium**:
  - Smooth spring physics and fluid card transitions.
  - Curated, high-contrast dark theme tokens (`AppColors.background`, `AppColors.card`, `AppColors.emerald`).
  - Tactile haptic feedback (`HapticFeedback.lightImpact`).
  - High-fidelity imagery representing real human faces, campus locations, and physical venues.
